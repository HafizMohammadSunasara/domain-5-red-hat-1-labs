## Concepts that connected across labs

### 1. Persistence and recoverability are not synonyms

In Lab 11, persistent storage separates data lifetime from container lifetime. In Lab 12, a logical SQL dump enables data recovery into another instance. A volume without a recoverable backup is not automatically a disaster-recovery plan.

### 2. Container diagnostics build toward orchestration diagnostics

Lab 13–15 move from `podman logs/inspect/stats/exec` to understanding networking, process behavior and VS Code remote debugging. Lab 18–19 extend the troubleshooting mindset to Kubernetes resources, events, Pods, PVCs and DNS.

### 3. Multi-container configuration is different from application reliability

Compose starts multiple services and can control dependency ordering, but service readiness needs more than startup ordering. Similarly, creating two MySQL StatefulSet Pods does not create automatic database replication or high availability.

### 4. Stable identity is a Kubernetes workload design choice

A single standalone Pod in Lab 18 and a StatefulSet in Lab 19 use different controllers and lifecycle expectations. StatefulSet ordinal names (`mysql-0`, `mysql-1`) and volume claim templates give independent storage to each member.
